## Why it matters in the plan

The plan treats digital and intelligent capabilities as a layer that reaches across manufacturing, services, infrastructure, and public administration. Computing power, data, algorithms, and artificial intelligence are discussed together rather than as a single isolated sector.

That framing matters: the digital economy is presented as an enabling architecture for productivity and industrial upgrading. The “AI+” agenda connects research and models to applications, while industrial software and intelligent manufacturing connect the digital layer to physical production.

## What the plan calls for

- Strengthen computing power, data infrastructure, algorithms, and AI innovation.
- Advance intelligent manufacturing, industrial software, and digital transformation.
- Expand AI application scenarios through the AI+ Action Initiative.
