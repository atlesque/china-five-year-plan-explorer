## Why it matters in the plan

The plan places high-end new materials in a list of industrial foundations. The examples range from specialty steels and high-temperature alloys to ultra-high-purity metals, advanced ceramics, polymers, composites, rare-earth materials, and frontier materials.

Materials sit upstream of many other priorities in the outline. They are less visible to a general reader than finished products, but they help explain how high-end equipment, energy systems, transport, and strategic emerging industries can be supported.

## What the plan calls for

- Strengthen high-end new-material development and industrialization.
- Improve materials used in major technical equipment and advanced manufacturing.
- Build capability across metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, and frontier materials.
