## Why it matters in the plan

The outline groups several air-and-space capabilities into its emerging-industry agenda: commercial aerospace, domestically produced large aircraft, and low-altitude equipment are each highlighted in Box 3. Infrastructure provisions add satellite internet and low-altitude infrastructure to the picture.

Read together, these passages describe an ecosystem rather than a single vehicle category. Space-based connectivity, aircraft manufacturing, unmanned systems, and the infrastructure that enables safe low-altitude activity appear as connected development areas.

## What the plan calls for

- Develop commercial aerospace and domestically produced large aircraft.
- Advance low-altitude equipment and related infrastructure.
- Strengthen satellite internet and other new-infrastructure capabilities.
