## Why it matters in the plan

Intelligent connected new-energy vehicles are named directly among the strategic emerging industries. The category brings together electrification and intelligent connectivity, making the vehicle part of a wider energy, mobility, and information system.

The plan’s related energy and infrastructure language suggests that progress depends on more than vehicle production: batteries, charging, networks, intelligent driving, and transport-system coordination all matter to the surrounding ecosystem.

## What the plan calls for

- Promote intelligent connected new-energy vehicles and next-generation batteries.
- Continue transport electrification and the build-out of supporting infrastructure.
- Develop intelligent transport capabilities alongside advanced manufacturing.
