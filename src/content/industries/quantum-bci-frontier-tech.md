## Why it matters in the plan

Quantum technology and brain-computer interfaces are named in the future-industries section and in the list of ten highlighted tracks. Their placement signals a long-horizon development area rather than a conventional sector forecast.

The plan pairs frontier technology with basic research, innovation platforms, demonstration, and commercialization pathways. That combination is a useful reminder that the outline describes policy attention and capability-building, not guaranteed near-term outcomes.

## What the plan calls for

- Advance quantum technology and related basic and applied research.
- Develop brain-computer interfaces and explore application pathways.
- Support demonstrations and commercialization where frontier technologies become mature enough.
