## Why it matters in the plan

Green hydrogen is one of the ten new industries and tracks highlighted in the plan. It appears alongside storage and transport questions, as well as possible applications in green ammonia, green methanol, sustainable aviation fuel, and industry.

The category is best read as a pipeline. The plan’s language brings together production from renewable energy, the infrastructure required to move and store hydrogen, and the industrial processes that could use new fuels.

## What the plan calls for

- Develop renewable-hydrogen production and related storage and transport systems.
- Explore green ammonia, green methanol, and sustainable aviation fuel.
- Expand clean-energy applications in industry and other hard-to-abate areas.
